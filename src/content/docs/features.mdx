## Streaming

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Multi-Source</h4>
    <p>Streams from multiple providers simultaneously. If one goes down, another takes over instantly — zero downtime.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Quality Selection</h4>
    <p>Choose your resolution per episode. From data-saver 360p to crisp 1080p.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Subtitle Customization</h4>
    <p>Font, size, color, stroke, background, position — make subtitles yours.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Playback Controls</h4>
    <p>Speed control (0.25x–3x), 10s skip forward/rewind, Picture-in-Picture on desktop.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Auto-Play</h4>
    <p>Current episode ends, next one starts. Seamless binge sessions.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Server Selection</h4>
    <p>Choose between sub and dub. Pick your preferred server per episode.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Library & Browsing

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Continue Watching</h4>
    <p>Picks up exactly where you left off. Progress saved locally, survives app restarts.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Offline Downloads</h4>
    <p>Queue up episodes with quality selection. Download once, watch anywhere without internet.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Search</h4>
    <p>Instant search across the full anime library. Filter within episode ranges on the watch page.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Discover</h4>
    <p>Trending spotlight with ratings, sub/dub counts, and quality tags. Curated sections for every taste.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Schedule</h4>
    <p>See what's airing and when. Never miss a new episode drop.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Info Page</h4>
    <p>Full metadata — synopsis, seasons, characters, sub/dub counts, related titles.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Sync & Tracking

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>AniList</h4>
    <p>OAuth login via your device browser. Sync your list status, progress, and scores.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>MyAnimeList</h4>
    <p>Secure OAuth flow with full list management from inside the app.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-item">
    <h4>Local-First</h4>
    <p>Everything works without an account. Continue watching, downloads, preferences — all stored on your device.</p>
  </div>
</div>
