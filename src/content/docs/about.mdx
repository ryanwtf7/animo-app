## The Idea

ANIMO started as a simple idea: anime streaming shouldn't be painful. No shady popups, no buffering hell, no "this video is not available in your region." Just pick a show and watch it.

## What ANIMO Does

ANIMO pulls streams from multiple sources and gives you one clean interface to browse, watch, and download. If one source goes down, another takes over automatically. No manual switching, no broken links.

## How It Works

Search for any anime, hit play, and ANIMO handles the rest — server selection, quality picking, subtitle loading. Everything happens behind the scenes so you can just watch.

Downloads work the same way. Pick your quality, save episodes locally, watch without internet. Your progress syncs automatically so you never lose your place.
